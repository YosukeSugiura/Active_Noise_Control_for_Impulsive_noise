Fig. 3. Noise reduction with the coefficients frozen at each time after the switch of the noise source (median over 200 runs: ten noise types and 20 seeds). The bands show the interquartile range for the proposed algorithm and CohVSS-FxNLMS. The time axis is linear up to 0.55 s and logarithmic thereafter. The vertical dotted lines mark 0.13 s and 0.51 s.

Fig. 4. Difference in the noise reduction with frozen coefficients between the proposed algorithm and CohVSS-FxNLMS for each noise type (mean of the paired differences over 20 seeds). Negative values indicate a larger noise reduction for the proposed algorithm. A dagger marks a 95% confidence interval that includes zero.
