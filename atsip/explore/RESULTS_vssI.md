# RESULTS vssI: step-size rules (delayless FD-FxNLMS)

L1 = ANR mean in dB, lower (more negative) is better. Means over non-diverged runs; n = runs used, div = diverged runs. Path change columns: 15-17 s / 25-30 s / whole.

## Observations

- Divergences: 0 of 10 jobs x 15 rules (no NaN runs).
- Gaussian whole: SF-I variants (-30.71 to -30.74) and SF-V2 (-30.72) are within 0.03 dB; SF-I beats fixed SF+ramp (-30.65) by about 0.1 dB. GS-T (-29.72) and the Mix rules (-29.34 to -29.82, Mix-BG0 -23.64) are worse than their SF counterparts.
- SF-IE (Euclidean) and SF-I (Phi) differ by at most 0.04 dB on whole-run means in each group.
- SF-Y (-30.08 Gaussian, -28.92 alpha 1.45) is worse than SF-I; its mu grows to about 0.5 (cap binds) in the Gaussian G0 trace.
- Mix-BG0 is far worse in every group; on the path change its 15-17 s window is +0.30 dB. Its applied mu sits at the 0.5/g_n cap (0.5027 in G0) for several probes.
- Path change: all SF integral variants give whole-run -29.6 to -29.8, against -29.69 for SF-V2. Window 15-17 s ranges -18.98 (g.013) to -21.72 (g.05); SF-Y gives -25.65.
- Integral mu on G0 decays to about 0.003 by block 3700 (SF-I g.026), versus 0.016 for SF-V2 at the same block.
- Update-only timing: GS-V2 is cheapest (0.047 ms/block). The dense Phi product adds cost: SF-I 0.242 vs SF-V2 0.158 ms/block.
- These numbers come from 2-3 seeds per group, so differences below about 0.05 dB are not resolved.
