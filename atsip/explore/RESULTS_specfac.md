## Conclusion

1. SF with a step control (m0=3, T=2 to 4, eps 1e-2) gives the best white whole mean (-29.50 dB, against -29.36 for GS and -28.70 for W0.5), but only 0.04 to 0.14 dB ahead of GS, and GS stays better on a1.2 (-28.83 against -28.4 to -28.6) and colored (-24.92 against about -24.6) whole means.
2. SF costs 1.27x W0.5 per block, and the chosen white mu sits at the lowest grid value (0.05), so a smaller mu may do better; the 0.6 maximum stable mu (against 0.4 for W0.5 and GS) is a grid result on ten sources, with no significance test.
3. Constant-Srr reduction and the prototype numbers (-29.00/-28.08/-29.24) reproduce; the eps and T differences inside the SF rows are below 0.1 dB and are not separated from noise here.
