## Conclusion

1. MFBLMS does not beat GS or SF. White whole L1 is -28.91 dB against -29.36 (GS) and -29.35 (SF), and white whole L2 is -26.04 dB against -29.41 and -29.38, so it trails by 0.44 to 0.45 dB in L1 and about 3.4 dB in L2. Its max stable mu is 0.2 (B0 and SF: 0.6), with 24 of 90 sweep runs diverging, and it costs about 1.14 times B0 per block (0.289 against 0.254 ms, within the roughly 10 percent timing spread).
2. The only end-state gain is MFBLMS+ramp: white last10 L1 is -30.01 dB against -29.88 for SF+ramp (0.13 dB on six sources, no significance test), bought with first5 at -22.93 against -27.94 dB. Its chosen mu, 0.02, is the grid floor, so its numbers may be step-limited.
3. Switch (-29.06 dB) and Wu-Yang (-29.15 at gamma_max 1, -28.57 at gamma_max 10) trail SF+ramp (-29.50) on white whole L1, and on the inclass Gaussian, step and burst inputs they trail SF+ramp by 0.45 to 0.81 dB in L1 whole.
4. On the path change the reset-capable methods do not win uniformly. Paired by source, WY g=10 beats SF over 15-17 s on all three sources (by 0.9, 3.9 and 2.6 dB; mean -17.4 against -14.9 dB) but is 0.5 to 1.2 dB worse than SF over 25-30 s. Switch is 6.4 to 8.1 dB worse than SF over 15-17 s and 0.1 to 0.2 dB better over 25-30 s. WY g=1 is 0.9 to 2.8 dB worse than SF over 15-17 s and mixed over 25-30 s. GS is about 1.3 dB worse than SF over 25-30 s.
5. Caveats: the Switch threshold is at the top of its grid (1.0 dB), so larger values were not tried. The path-change test has three sources, no significance tests are run, timing is noisy on 2 vCPUs, and the mechanism behind the transient differences was not isolated.

## Deviations from the brief

- Detector: J is the EMA (0.05 per block) of the block error power in dB, and the stagnation test is the decrease over the last 32 blocks (dJ < TAU dB). The brief's per-block relative dJ/J was dominated by heavy-tailed block power: its median stayed near 0.04 in every segment of the run (`python3 atsip/explore/mfblms.py diag` now prints the dB statistics). The environment-change test is J - min(J since reset/switch) > 6.02 dB, the 4x ratio in the brief.
- Tuning: TAU for Switch and WY was chosen on a145 by the lowest whole L1 (a145 is also one of the ten sources in condition A, as the brief implies). The grid is {0, 0.3, 1.0} dB.
- Gamma step: Delta gamma = 0.1 gamma_max (the brief left the size open).
- Analytic check: the expected-value fixed point is exact in expectation for white input (bias -278 dB). No long-run empirical check of the weights was run.
