## Conclusion

1. The best online-estimated config (L=64, beta=0.97, g0 mode A, monotone, m0=3, mu=0.05) reaches white whole -29.31 dB, against -29.36 dB for the manual ramp (T=4, m0=3) and -28.70 dB for W0.5, so the estimator matches the manual ramp and beats W0.5 by about 0.6 dB, with 6 vs 11 divergent sweep runs.
2. The estimated w saturates at 1 within about 3 to 4 s (faster than the 4 s ramp) on both traced sources, so the schedule is effectively a fast W0.5 with an m0 boost; the decay-rate signal does not stay informative enough to hold w between 0 and 1.
3. Under the abrupt change, w did not drop (it stayed at 1.000), so the estimator gave no reaction; its ANR recovery (-1.65 dB recovered minus pre) matches the manual ramp (-1.63 dB) and is worse than W0.5 (-2.63 dB), so I see no evidence that the online estimate adds adaptivity.
